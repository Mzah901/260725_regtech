---
title: Sideways is still progress.
rank: 7
tag: LIFE
---
Life often moves like a chart going sideways: no clear rise and no clear fall, for a long time. Then one day changes things, for better or worse. The job during the sideways stretch is to keep working.

Think of moving averages. If your last 50 days are better than your last 200, the trend follows. If the last 50 were bad, so is the trend, until you change them.

The return on that work comes back as value ([[Equivalent exchange.]]), and the work most worth doing is building ([[Lawyers should build.]]).
