Engineers compete by building: bridges, rockets, chips, software. Legal professionals too often compete over who wins a case or a seat, rather than over who makes the law work better for the people who have to follow it.

Legal work deserves the same healthy competition engineering has, hackathons included: tools that make the law easier to know, to follow and to check.

This lab is my small attempt, starting with [[Law is a database problem.]] Building takes time before it pays back ([[Equivalent exchange.]]).
