Regulatory AI Labs

Applying AI to Indonesian regulation using public sources only. Each experiment publishes its data, method, sources and known errors.

About

I trained in law at Universitas Gadjah Mada and work on Indonesian regulatory compliance. I have no formal computing background; this lab is where I learn to build and evaluate AI tools for the problems I know from practice: finding obligations in regulations, tracking amendments, and mapping how rules refer to each other.

Experiments

  1. 11 Oct 2026 Legal Industry Map 27 legal-tech companies in Indonesia, ASEAN and global markets, with disclosed funding and sources. Pilot.

Responsible AI

Every experiment follows the same rules, written down before any code:

  • Public sources only. Regulations from peraturan.go.id, JDIH portals, Pasal.id, official gazettes and published court decisions. No private or workplace data.
  • A human reviews everything. AI output is a draft until I have checked it. Each experiment log records what I reviewed.
  • Measured, not eyeballed. Results are scored against a sample I check by hand, with error counts, not "it looks right".
  • Limits stated up front. Each experiment lists its sources, tools, models and known errors.

The full rules are in CLAUDE.md; every experiment's log is in experiments/.
