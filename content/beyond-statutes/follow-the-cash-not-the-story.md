---
title: Follow the cash, not the story.
rank: 6
tag: FINANCE
---
A company can report profit that never arrives as cash. Revenue can be recognised before the customer pays, and operating cash flow shows what actually came in. Reading the three statements together (profit and loss, balance sheet, cash flow) beats trusting any one of them.

People and projects are the same: judge them by what they deliver, not by what they promise ([[The law exists because promises break.]]). Value that is real eventually shows up ([[Equivalent exchange.]]).
