I'm Muhammad Hamzah Al Faruq. I studied constitutional law at Universitas Gadjah Mada, drafted regional regulations for local governments, and now work in Indonesian regulatory compliance, where I test and build with AI. This page is the record. In the 3D scene on the home page, the trophies, the books under the desk and the pillars in the desert open these sections.
