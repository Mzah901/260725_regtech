Beyond Statutes

Notes from my journal. Law has never stopped evolving, from canon law to code, and neither should the way we work with it.

  1. CH.01

    Law is a database problem.

    Regulations and court decisions change constantly. AI can already handle everyday legal questions if you give it the right documents. The hard part is knowing that what you gave it is complete, correct and still in force. That is the problem this lab works on.

  2. CH.02

    Evidence before norms.

    Good policy is built on evidence and measured impact, not on legal reasoning alone, and public participation should be meaningful, not a formality. The values I work by: rational autonomy, evidence-based and impact-driven thinking, integrity and justice.

  3. CH.03

    The law exists because promises break.

    Contract law and its relatives exist because people cannot simply be trusted to keep their word. So I believe in the rule of law over the rule of individuals, and I try to do what I promise, or say early when I cannot.

  4. CH.04

    Lawyers should build.

    Engineers compete by building bridges, rockets and software. Legal work deserves the same healthy competition: tools that make the law easier to know and to follow.

  5. CH.05

    Learn slowly, then master.

    Finishing a course quickly means little if you cannot explain it afterwards. I would rather learn slowly, fail, and end up understanding what matters and what does not.

  6. CH.06

    Hype or real use case?

    Every few years a new story promises to replace everything. The only reliable way to tell hype from a real use case is to become the expert, or to work closely with one.

  7. CH.07

    Sideways is still progress.

    Life often moves like a chart going sideways. The job in those stretches is to keep working. If your last 50 days beat your last 200, the trend follows.
