---
title: The law exists because promises break.
rank: 3
tag: LAW
---
Contract law, security law and much of the rest exist because people cannot simply be trusted to keep their word. Verbal agreements get broken without evidence or argument, and promises about pay, deadlines and partnerships quietly lapse.

That is why I believe in the rule of law over the rule of individuals. It is also a personal rule: do what you promise, change it only when something truly outside your control happens, and take responsibility when you still cannot deliver.

Judging people by what they deliver rather than what they promise is the same idea applied to money: [[Follow the cash, not the story.]] And rules that are enforced should also be good rules: [[Evidence before norms.]]
