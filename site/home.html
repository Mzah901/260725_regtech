House rules

  • Public sources only: peraturan.go.id, JDIH portals, Pasal.id, official gazettes, published court decisions. No private or workplace data.
  • A human reviews everything. AI output is a draft until I have checked it.
  • Measured, not eyeballed. Results are scored against a hand-checked sample, with error counts.
  • Limits stated up front: every experiment lists its sources, tools, models and known errors.
