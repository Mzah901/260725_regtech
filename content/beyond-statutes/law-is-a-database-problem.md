title: Law is a database problem.
rank: 1
tag: LAW
---
Regulations and court decisions change constantly. AI can already handle everyday legal questions if you give it the right regulations, case law and documents. The hard part is knowing that what you gave it is complete, correct and still in force.

That makes the modern legal problem a database problem more than a reasoning problem: which version of a rule applies today, what amended it, and what it refers to. It is also why I think compliance is underrated. The work is in keeping the map current, not in reading any single statute.

This lab works on that problem, with every result checked by hand ([[Evidence before norms.]]). It is also my own answer to [[Lawyers should build.]]
